lightphotos

A fast, Lightroom-lite photo culling and develop tool written in Rust. Open a folder to browse thumbnails, rate and filter them, then open any photo to zoom, pan and edit.

What it does

  • Grid view for culling: rate, filter and move through a shoot from the keyboard.
  • Loupe view for editing, with zoom, pan, Auto Tone and JPEG export.
  • Reads JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC and camera RAW files.
  • Ratings and edits are saved to a .lightphotos folder next to your photos, so the originals are never modified.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your photos and edits stay on your computer.

Runs on

macOS, Windows, Linux and the browser. HEIC and face/blink scoring are macOS-only.

lightdraw

A multiplayer whiteboard where you build in 3D. A document is also a presentation: viewers look into a glowing hologram while the camera flies between scenes.

What it does

  • Draw, move, extrude and connect shapes on a snapping 3D grid.
  • Every shape can hold text, and arrows connect objects.
  • Save a selection as a reusable component. Placed copies stay linked to the original, as in Figma.
  • Edit together in real time over WebSockets. Sync follows Figma’s design: last write wins per property, ordered by the server.
  • All Rust: a wgpu renderer compiled to WebAssembly, so large canvases stay fast.

Runs on

Runs in the browser. Rooms are hosted on Cloudflare.

lightandseek

A multiplayer hide-and-seek game. Everyone joins a room by its link. Hiders scatter while the seeker waits blind, then the seeker hunts until the clock runs out.

What it does

  • Three maps: a park, a city and a furnished house.
  • The seeker is a little faster, and catches a hider by getting close while facing them with a clear line of sight.
  • Up to 8 players per room, and bots can fill the seeker or hider spots.
  • The host sets the rules in the lobby. Reload mid-round and you rejoin where you were.
  • All Rust: a wgpu renderer on WebGPU or WebGL2, with client-side prediction so movement feels instant.

Runs on

Runs in the browser. Rooms are hosted on Cloudflare.

lightdisks

A desktop app that shows where your disk space goes. Pick a folder and lightdisks scans it, then draws everything inside as a shaded treemap where each tile’s area is its size.

What it does

  • Cushion-shaded treemap, colored by top-level folder, so nested folders read at a glance.
  • Click to zoom into a folder and navigate back out.
  • A sidebar lists the largest folders, with a legend of file types by size.
  • Hide a folder to see the rest more clearly, and restore it later.
  • Move files and folders to the Trash from inside the app.

Runs on

Desktop app written in Rust (egui).

lightremote

An iPhone remote for Sony cameras over Wi-Fi, built for the A7II. It shows live view and controls exposure, shoots, and keeps a GPS track so your photos can be geotagged later.

What it does

  • Live view with a histogram, focus peaking and a zoom loupe.
  • Exposure controls, plus back-button autofocus that holds while you press.
  • Shooting modes: single, self-timer, interval, bulb and movie.
  • A gallery of preview shots that you can save to Photos.
  • Records a GPS track, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. A Mac helper uses it to geotag the full-size RAW and JPEG files.

Runs on

iPhone app written in Swift and SwiftUI.
